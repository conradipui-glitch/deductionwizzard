A documentary sample, **not** a closed criminal case or a measured probability of a conspiracy.

## Interpretation caveats

Heatmap colors are manually annotated `supports | contradicts | compatible | unknown`. They **do not** represent calibrated probabilities, peer consensus, multiple independent eyewitnesses or verified causal mechanisms. A source's date and a reported event's date are separate. Scholarly comments quote other sources and must not be tallied as extra witnesses.
